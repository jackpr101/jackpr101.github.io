Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 1 November 2026

This policy explains how Jacent Labs ("we", "us" or "our") handles personal information when you visit www.jacentlabs.com or contact us. Our Small Things app has its own privacy policy.

1. Information We Collect

When you use our contact form or email us, we receive your name, your email address and anything else you choose to include in your message.

When you visit this website, our hosting provider records technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the date and time of your visit. An IP address can count as personal information.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you send us to reply to your enquiry and to provide any information you have asked for. We do this because we have a legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us, or because you have asked us about our software or services.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Who Handles Your Information

We use the following providers to run this website and our email:

  • Formspree processes messages sent through our contact form. It may show a Google reCAPTCHA check to block spam. See Formspree's Privacy Policy.
  • Zoho provides our email, so it stores the messages we send and receive. See Zoho's Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub hosts this website and keeps the technical visitor logs described above. We do not have access to those logs. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

These providers may store information outside the UK, including in the United States.

4. Cookies and Analytics

This website does not set its own cookies and does not use analytics or advertising trackers.

5. How Long We Keep Your Information

We keep your messages only for as long as we need them to deal with your enquiry and any follow-up.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no method of sending or storing information over the internet is completely secure.

7. Your Data Protection Rights

You can ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. You can also object to how we use it. To do any of these, please contact us.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact us at contact@jacentlabs.com.
